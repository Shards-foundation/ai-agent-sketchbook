# Product Requirement Document (PRD) Template

## 1. Executive Summary
*   **Project Name**: [Project Name]
*   **Status**: [Draft/In Review/Approved]
*   **Owner**: [Agent/Human Name]
*   **Objective**: A brief overview of what this product/feature aims to achieve.

## 2. Problem Statement
*   **Context**: What is the current situation?
*   **Pain Points**: What specific problems are being addressed?
*   **Target Audience**: Who is this for?

## 3. Goals & Success Metrics
*   **Primary Goal**: The main objective.
*   **Secondary Goals**: Supporting objectives.
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**: How will success be measured?

## 4. Functional Requirements
*   **Requirement 1**: Description and priority.
*   **Requirement 2**: Description and priority.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
*   **Performance**: Latency, throughput, etc.
*   **Security**: Authentication, authorization, data protection.
*   **Scalability**: How the system handles growth.

## 6. User Stories
*   As a [User Role], I want to [Action] so that [Benefit].

## 7. Constraints & Assumptions
*   **Constraints**: Technical, budgetary, or time limitations.
*   **Assumptions**: What are we taking for granted?

## 8. Roadmap & Milestones
*   **Phase 1**: [Description and Date]
*   **Phase 2**: [Description and Date]

## 9. Risk Assessment
*   **Risk 1**: Mitigation strategy.
*   **Risk 2**: Mitigation strategy.
